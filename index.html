About me

Hi, I'm Niel

I’m an aspiring full-stack developer who likes turning problems into working software. I’ve just finished A levels in Maths, Physics and Computer Science, and I’m now on a full-stack development course, building the skills to take ideas from a blank file to a live web app.

My first big build was a game in Python and Pygame for my A level coursework. I planned it, broke it into pieces and kept going when the code fought back. It showed me how much I enjoy turning an idea into something that works, and it’s what pushed me towards full-stack development.

A week as an IT technician also gave me a look at how technology is used day to day and how much people rely on it working.

I’m looking for opportunities to keep learning, work with a team and build things people actually use. Have a look around, and feel free to get in touch.
